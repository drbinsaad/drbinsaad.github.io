# Chronic rhinosinusitis

#seedling

*(Example concept note stub — a note is allowed to start this small.)*

Inflammation of the nose and paranasal sinuses ≥ 12 weeks. Phenotypes: with polyps (CRSwNP) / without (CRSsNP); endotype thinking (type 2 inflammation) increasingly guides biologics.

## Assessment
- Symptoms: [[SNOT-22]]
- Endoscopy: Lund-Kennedy score
- CT: [[Lund-Mackay score]]
