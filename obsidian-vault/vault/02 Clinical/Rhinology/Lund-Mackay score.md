# Lund-Mackay score

#evergreen

*(Example concept note — shows the format. Replace or extend freely.)*

CT staging system for chronic rhinosinusitis. Each sinus system scored 0–2 (0 = clear, 1 = partial opacification, 2 = total), ostiomeatal complex scored 0 or 2. Both sides scored separately → total 0–24.

## Key points
- Widely used for research grading and pre-FESS documentation.
- Poor correlation with symptom scores like [[SNOT-22]] — radiology ≠ patient experience.
- My online calculator: <https://drbinsaad.github.io/lund-mackay.html>
