# 🎓 Teaching

Lectures, tutorials, and resident teaching. Build a talk by **transcluding** concept notes
(`![[Cholesteatoma#Notes]]`) instead of copying — one source of truth, and your teaching improves
your own notes.

Use the **Teaching note** template. Appears in [[Library]] under `teaching`.

Tip: the **Slides** core plugin turns a note with `---` separators into a presentation.
