# 🩺 Cases

De-identified teaching cases. Each links to the concept notes it illustrates, so your clinical
experience feeds your knowledge graph.

> ⚠️ **Never record patient-identifiable data** — no names, MRN, DOB, or unique details.
> Log the *lesson*, using age/sex/pathology at most. This vault is personal knowledge, never a
> medical record.

Use the **Case note** template. Cases appear in [[Library]] under `case`.
