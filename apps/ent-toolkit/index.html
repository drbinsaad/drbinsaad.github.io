Qualified ENT clinicians

Focused tools. Transparent references.

ENT Toolkit is a focused offline utility and reference for otolaryngology. It combines transparent calculations, equipment sizing references, classification atlases and searchable evidence in one private on-device workspace.

iOS · Offline · No account
01

Reference-first

Sources, limitations and calculation logic remain visible so clinicians can independently verify the basis of each result.

02

Session-only context

Age, weight, sex and recent calculations remain only for the current session. The app is not a patient record.

03

Private by design

No sign-in, analytics, advertising, cloud service or network request. The developer collects no data.

Educational reference—not a medical service

ENT Toolkit does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or replace training, local protocols, manufacturer instructions or clinical judgment. It contains no drug-dosage tools, patient logbook, user accounts or patient-instruction service.
