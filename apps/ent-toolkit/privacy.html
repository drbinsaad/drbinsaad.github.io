ENT Toolkit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 August 2026

ENT Toolkit is an offline reference and calculation app for qualified healthcare professionals. It does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or provide patient-specific advice, and it does not replace clinical judgment, institutional protocols, or emergency care.

ENT Toolkit collects no data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service, and the app makes no network requests. Nothing you enter is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

What the app stores

Everything the app saves stays in its private storage on your device:

  • Favourite tools, so your most-used tools stay at the top.
  • Recent calculation history, so you can review a recent result.
  • Language, appearance and display preferences.
  • Work contact details and shift checklist items that you choose to enter.
  • Values you type into a calculator, used only to compute the result you asked for.

This information is never uploaded, never shared, and never sold. Deleting the app removes all of it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask you to sign in.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any identifier.
  • It does not collect or store patient information, and you should not enter any patient-identifiable information into it. The app actively blocks obvious identifiers, such as record numbers and email addresses, before they can be saved.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, location, or health data.
  • It does not require an internet connection.

Third parties

ENT Toolkit uses no third-party service providers, processors, or analytics platforms of any kind. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Patient information

Do not enter patient names, medical record numbers, national IDs, phone numbers, dates of birth, or any other identifying information into the app. Enter only the de-identified measurements a calculation requires. The app displays this reminder on first launch and screens free-text fields for obvious identifiers before saving.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for use by qualified healthcare professionals. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and the effective date above will be revised.
